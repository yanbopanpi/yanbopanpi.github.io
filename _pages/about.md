I am an astronomy PhD student at the University of Virginia. I study dark matter and galaxy evolution through stellar populations in nearby galaxies. I also develop statistical tools that help astronomers extract more information from limited telescope observations.

## Research Interests
- Observational Near-Field Cosmology
- Ultra-faint Dwarf Galaxies
- Stellar Dynamics
- Statistical and Computational Applications on Astrophysics

## Research highlights

- **Mapping Sagittarius:** Using stellar motions and chemistry to investigate the mass structure of a satellite dwarf galaxy undergoing tidal disruption.
- **Developing HARLEE:** Building software to help astronomers choose informative follow-up observations of binary stars.
- **Recent publication:** My first-author [JWST study of WASP-52b](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ae9cbc) explores how stellar activity affects the interpretation of an exoplanet’s atmosphere.
