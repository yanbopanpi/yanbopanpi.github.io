## About Me

![Running Picture]({{ '/images/marathon.jpg' | relative_url }}){: .align-right width="320px"}

I am from [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo), a vibrant coastal city in southeastern China. My academic path began at the University of Wisconsin-Madison 🦡, where I spent my first year studying along the picturesque shores of [Lake Mendota](https://lakeshorepreserve.wisc.edu/visit/places/lake-mendota/). I liked the weather so much that I transferred to the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor ☃️ to complete my undergraduate studies. Now I am a graduate student at the University of Virginia.

Outside of academics and research, I enjoy:
- Distance running 🏃‍♂️
- Playing table tennis 🏓
- Kayaking on the Huron River 🚣‍♂️

When the weather is pleasant, you’ll often find me running through the Charlottesville area. I also love tennis, table tennis, and hiking.
